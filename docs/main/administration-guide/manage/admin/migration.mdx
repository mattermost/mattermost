---
title: "Migration"
---
Moving an existing Mattermost deployment onto different infrastructure, and getting your data out of one.

- [Bulk export tool](/administration-guide/manage/bulk-export-tool) - Export users, teams, channels, and posts to a JSONL file.
- [Migrate from MySQL to PostgreSQL](/administration-guide/manage/admin/postgres-migration) - Move your database, using the migration assist tool or by hand.
- [Migrate to FIPS-compliant images](/administration-guide/manage/admin/fips-migration) - Switch an existing Docker or Kubernetes deployment to the FIPS-compliant Mattermost images.

Moving to Mattermost from Slack, Rocket.Chat, or GitLab Omnibus is covered in [migrating to Mattermost](/administration-guide/onboard/migrating-to-mattermost), alongside [bulk loading data](/administration-guide/onboard/bulk-loading-data) and the [migration announcement email template](/administration-guide/onboard/migration-announcement-email).
