Use the hosted **1-hour cloud preview** when you want to explore Mattermost in a live environment without installing anything.

## How it works

1. Open the sign-up form on mattermost.com (button below).
2. Enter your business details and use case.
3. After Marketo lead capture succeeds, you are redirected to the Customer Portal with `flow=preview`.
4. The portal provisions a preview workspace and signs you in for about one hour.
