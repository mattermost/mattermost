Mattermost extends Microsoft Teams, Microsoft 365, and Entra ID for work that needs more control than everyday collaboration tools provide, including sensitive data, incident response outside your primary systems, and private, classified, or disconnected networks. Your people stay in Teams and Outlook, while sensitive work runs in a Mattermost deployment that you own and control.

The following capabilities are available:

## Sovereign Collaboration

Organizations bound by data sovereignty or compliance rules often can't run sensitive collaboration in a shared cloud.

**Benefits**

- **Deploy where your data must stay.** Run Mattermost on-premises, in a private or sovereign cloud, on Azure, or in an air-gapped network. See [deployment options](https://docs.mattermost.com/deployment-guide/server/deploy-server#deployment-options). Air-gapped deployment workflows are an Enterprise Advanced capability.
- **Stay in Teams and Outlook.** Embed Mattermost in Microsoft 365, Teams, and Outlook with [Mattermost Mission Collaboration for Microsoft](https://docs.mattermost.com/integrations-guide/mattermost-mission-collaboration-for-m365) (Beta). Users sign in automatically, and mentions and direct messages appear in the Teams Activity Feed. Available on Entry, Enterprise, and Enterprise Advanced.
- **Keep control of your data.** Messages and files stay in storage you control. Apply [legal hold](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/comply/legal-hold) and [data retention policies](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/comply/data-retention-policy) (Enterprise and above), and use [granular system permissions](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/onboard/advanced-permissions) and [delegated administration](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/onboard/delegated-granular-administration) to control who can do what.

## Out-of-Band Incident Response

During an incident, Microsoft 365 may be unavailable, or the systems under attack may not be trustworthy.

**Benefits**

- **Keep working when primary systems are down.** Run a separate, out-of-band Mattermost instance for incident communication and coordination.
- **Follow a clear plan.** Use [Playbooks](https://docs.mattermost.com/end-user-guide/workflow-automation/workflow-automation-index) for repeatable triage, escalation, and resolution.
- **Keep identity centralized.** Sign in with [Entra ID](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/onboard/sso-entraid) using OpenID Connect, or [SAML](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/onboard/sso-saml-entraid). Available on Professional and above.
- **Stay aware of enterprise activity.** [Microsoft Teams Sync](https://docs.mattermost.com/integrations-guide/microsoft-teams-sync) forwards Teams chat notifications to Mattermost. It requires an Azure OAuth app and a paid Azure subscription for metered APIs, and is available on Entry, Enterprise, and Enterprise Advanced.
- **Meet quickly.** Start Teams meetings from a Mattermost channel with [Microsoft Teams Meetings](https://docs.mattermost.com/integrations-guide/microsoft-teams-meetings) (Entry, Professional, Enterprise, and Enterprise Advanced), and connect your calendar with [Microsoft Calendar](https://docs.mattermost.com/integrations-guide/microsoft-calendar).
- **Protect mobile notifications.** Use [ID-only push notifications](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/configure/push-notification-server-configuration-settings#id-only-push-notifications) so notifications carry no message content (Enterprise and above).

## External Collaboration with Full Control

Partners, contractors, and allies often aren't on your Microsoft tenant, which can push teams toward unmanaged tools.

**Benefits**

- **Share channels with partners.** Use [connected workspaces](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/onboard/connected-workspaces) to share channels between your Mattermost server and a partner's Mattermost server (Entry, Enterprise, and Enterprise Advanced).
- **Govern each side separately.** Each server controls its own channel permissions and access, and you can require [custom Terms of Service](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/comply/custom-terms-of-service) (Enterprise and above).

:::note
Connected workspaces link Mattermost servers to each other. They don't connect directly to Microsoft Teams tenants.
:::

## AI Inside Your Boundary

Teams that can't send prompts or data to outside services still need AI to move faster.

**Benefits**

- **Choose where models run.** [Connect agents](https://docs.mattermost.com/administration-guide/configure/agents-admin-guide) to Azure OpenAI or to models you host yourself through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
- **Work faster in channels and playbooks.** Use [Mattermost Agents](https://docs.mattermost.com/end-user-guide/agents) to summarize threads, answer questions, and automate tasks.
